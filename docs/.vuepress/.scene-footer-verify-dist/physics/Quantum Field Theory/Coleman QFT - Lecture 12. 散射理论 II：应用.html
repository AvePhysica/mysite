12.1 衰变

衰变过程就是指初态为单粒子态的跃迁过程。衰变的单位时间微分跃迁概率记为 dΓ\mathrm{d}\varGammadΓ，而总的单位时间跃迁概率记为 Γ\varGammaΓ。即

rest decay. prob.unit time=Γ=12m∑f∫∣Afi∣2D\dfrac{\text{rest decay. prob.}}{\text{unit time}}=\varGamma=\dfrac{1}{2m}\sum_f\int|\mathcal{A}_{fi}|^2D unit timerest decay. prob.​=Γ=2m1​f∑​∫∣Afi​∣2D
